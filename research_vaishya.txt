1. Agarwal vs Khandelwal vs Oswal vs Jaiswal:
Usually, these are distinct Vaishya (Baniya) communities with different mythological/historical origins:
- Agarwals -> Maharaja Agrasen (Agroha)
- Oswals -> Osian (Rajasthan), generally trace origin to Rajput/Kshatriya converts to Jainism under Acharya Ratnaprabhasuri.
- Khandelwals -> Khandela (Rajasthan), originating from Khandal Rishi or a king of Khandela.
- Jaiswals -> Jaisalmer or Jais (UP), claiming Rajput or separate origins.
- Maheshwaris -> Didwana (Rajasthan), tracing origin to Khandela and blessing of Lord Shiva (Mahesh).
So they DO NOT branch out of Agrasen's line. They are parallel communities.

2. Agrasen's descendants after 18 sons:
There is practically a gap in specific continuous genealogies for individual Agarwals going back 5000 years, except for the 18 gotras. Most modern Agarwal families trace their lineage back only a few centuries.
