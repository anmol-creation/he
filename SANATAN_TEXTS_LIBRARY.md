# The Sanatan Digital Library (Pustakalay)

An exhaustive, categorized directory of texts belonging to the vast Sanatan framework, encompassing Vedic, Puranic, Shramana (Jain/Buddhist), and Gurmat (Sikh) traditions, alongside regional folklore and philosophies.

## 1. SHRUTI (श्रुति - Direct Revelation)
*   **Vedas (4):** Rigveda, Yajurveda, Samaveda, Atharvaveda.
*   **Mukhya Upanishads (108+):** Isha, Kena, Katha, Prashna, Mundaka, Mandukya, Taittiriya, Aitareya, Chandogya, Brihadaranyaka.

## 2. SMRITI (स्मृति - Law & Tradition)
*   **Dharmashastras / Smritis:** Manu Smriti, Yajnavalkya Smriti, Parashara Smriti, Narada Smriti.
*   **Vedangas (6):** Shiksha, Kalpa, Vyakarana, Nirukta, Chhanda, Jyotisha.
*   **Upavedas (4):** Ayurveda, Dhanurveda, Gandharvaveda, Sthapatyaveda.

## 3. ITIHASA (इतिहास - History)
*   **Ramayana:** Valmiki, Adhyatma, Kamba, Ramcharitmanas, etc.
*   **Mahabharata:** Including Srimad Bhagavad Gita and Harivamsa.

## 4. PURANAS (पुराण - Ancient Lore & Cosmology)
*   **Maha Puranas (18):** Vishnu, Bhagavata, Shiva, Skanda, Padma, Garuda, Agni, Matsya, Kurma, Linga, etc.
*   **Upa Puranas (18+):** Samba, Kalki, Kalika, etc.
*   **Sthala Puranas:** Regional/Temple histories.

## 5. DARSHANA SHASTRA (दर्शन शास्त्र - Philosophy)
*   **Orthodox (Astika):** Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta.

## 6. AGAMAS & TANTRAS (आगम और तन्त्र)
*   **Vaishnava:** Pancharatra, Vaikhanasa.
*   **Shaiva:** Siddhanta Agamas, Kashmir Shaivism.
*   **Shakta:** Das Mahavidya texts, Saundarya Lahari.

## 7. SHRAMANA TRADITIONS (श्रमण - Jain & Bauddha)
*   **Bauddha (Buddhist) Texts:**
    * Tripitaka (Pali Canon): Vinaya, Sutta, Abhidhamma.
    * Mahayana Sutras: Lotus Sutra, Heart Sutra.
    * Jataka Tales (Past lives of Buddha containing ancient Indian lore).
    * Historical Chronicles: Mahavamsa, Dipavamsa.
*   **Jain Texts:**
    * Agamas (Svetambara & Digambara texts).
    * Kalpa Sutra (History of Tirthankaras).
    * Trishashthi Shalaka Purush Caritra (Deeds of 63 Illustrious Men - Jain Puranic equivalent).

## 8. GURMAT TRADITION (सिख - Sikh Texts)
*   **Sri Guru Granth Sahib (Adi Granth):** The central holy religious text of Sikhism.
*   **Dasam Granth:** Attributed to Guru Gobind Singh, containing texts like Chandi Di Var and Chaubis Avtar.
*   **Janam Sakhis:** Biographies of Guru Nanak.

## 9. BHASHYAS & NIBANDHAS (Commentaries)
*   Works of Adi Shankaracharya, Ramanujacharya, Madhvacharya, Chaitanya Mahaprabhu.

## 10. LOKA / KSHETRIYA SAHITYA (Regional/Folklore)
*   **Bhakti Texts:** Abhangs, Vachanas, Dohe (Kabir, Meera, Tulsidas).
*   **Sangam Literature:** Tolkappiyam, Tirukkural.
*   **Janjatiya (Tribal) Lore:** Santhal, Bhil, Gond oral histories.
