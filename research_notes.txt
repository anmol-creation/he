Note: Agrasen lineage claims he is the 34th descendant of Kush. We need to match the specific "Vallabhsen" or "Vallabha" in this lineage or right after this timeline.
Scribd document also mentions: Sudarshan -> Agnivarna -> Shighra -> Mohandas -> Neminath -> Vrand -> ... Vallabhsen.
This aligns slightly with "Sudarsana -> Agnivarna -> Sighraga (Shighra)" from the Puranic list (generations 36-38 of Rama).

According to Bharatendu Harishchandra:
- 1871 essay "Agarwalon ki Utpatti"
- Agrasen born around 3082 BC (End of Dwapar, start of Kali Yuga), contemporaneous to Krishna.
- Father: Suryavanshi Maharaja Vallabhsen of Pratapnagar (Rajasthan).
- Mother: Queen Bhagwati Devi.
- Source claimed by Bharatendu: "Sri Mahalakshmi Vrat Ki Katha" from a later part of Bhavishya Purana. However, scholars couldn't find this in published Bhavishya Puranas.
- In 1976, Satyaketu Vidyalankar published a copy of this text found in Bharatendu's personal library, but it lacked origin clues.

Folklore/Community:
- 18 sons -> 18 Gotras.
- 18 Rishis conducted Yagnas.
- Marriages: Madhavi (Nagvanshi daughter of King Kumud) and Nandini.
