Sacred Geography

The Cosmic Architecture & Puranic Worlds

🌌 ब्रह्मांड (The Cosmic Egg)

A

ccording to ancient Sanatan texts, the entire creation exists within a colossal, egg-shaped structure known as the Brahmanda (Brahma's Egg). It is believed that countless such universes exist. Within this specific Brahmanda, there are fourteen realms or Lokas, forming a grand cosmic hierarchy.

उर्ध्व लोक (Upper Worlds)

  • Satya Loka: Realm of Absolute Truth
  • Tapa Loka: Realm of Penance
  • Jana Loka: Realm of the Saints
  • Mahar Loka: Realm of Great Sages
  • Svar Loka: The Heavenly Realm (Indra's domain)
  • Bhuvar Loka: The Atmospheric Realm
  • Bhur Loka: The Earthly Realm

अधो लोक (Nether Worlds)

  • Atala: Realm devoid of form
  • Vitala: Realm of change
  • Sutala: The Great Abyss (Bali's domain)
  • Talatala: Under the Bottom level
  • Mahatala: The Great Bottom
  • Rasatala: Realm of the Demons
  • Patala: The Lowest Realm (Nagas)

The universe is enveloped by seven elements: Water, Fire, Air, Ether, Ego, Intelligence, and Pure Nature.

🪐 ज्योतिषचक्र (Galactic Systems)

Jyotischakra (The Wheel of Light)

All visible stars, constellations (Nakshatras), and galaxies in the night sky constitute the Jyotischakra. In the Bhagavata Purana, this vast stellar assembly is poetically visualized as a giant cosmic dolphin (Shishumara), with the Pole Star (Dhruva Tara) fixed at its tail—an ancient conceptualization of the Milky Way and the celestial sphere.

Bhu-Mandala (Planetary Orbits)

The expanse illuminated by the Sun and the Moon is defined as Bhu-Mandala. According to astronomical treatises like the Surya Siddhanta, planets orbit the sun in their designated paths (Kaksha), governed by the inexorable force of Time (Kala, akin to gravity).

✧

🌍 सप्त द्वीप (The Seven Continents)

The Surya Siddhanta explicitly describes the Earth as a round sphere ('Bhugol'). Puranic geography divides the Earth into seven massive landmasses or continents, known as the Sapta Dvipa. Each is encircled by a unique, vast ocean.

जम्बू Jambu Dvipa

The central continent (Eurasia/Asia). Encircled by the Salt Ocean.

प्लक्ष Plaksha Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Sugarcane Juice.

शाल्मली Shalmali Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Wine/Liquor.

कुश Kusha Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Clarified Butter (Ghee).

क्रौंच Krauncha Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Milk.

शाक Shaka Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Yogurt/Curd.

पुष्कर Pushkara Dvipa

Encircled by an ocean of Fresh Water.

Glory of Bharatavarsha

At the heart of Jambu Dvipa lies Mount Meru (the cosmic axis, often associated with the Pamir Knot). South of Meru and the Himalayas lies Bharatavarsha, the sacred land.

"उत्तरं यत्समुद्रस्य हिमाद्रेश्चैव दक्षिणम्।
वर्षं तद् भारतं नाम भारती यत्र सन्ततिः॥"

"The country that lies north of the ocean and south of the snowy mountains is called Bhāratam; there dwell the descendants of Bharata."
(Vishnu Purana 2.3.1)
