# Supportive Dynasties and Interconnected Lineages of Sri Lanka

This document preserves research on the various Indian lineages that interacted with, supported, or conflicted with the early Sinhala (Sri Lankan) royal lineage, specifically around the time of Prince Vijaya and the early Anuradhapura period.

## 1. The Pandyan Connection (Matrimonial Alliance)
*   **Role:** Sent the first royal brides to Sri Lanka.
*   **Details:** When Prince Vijaya settled in Sri Lanka, he requested a noble bride from the Pandyan King of Madhura (Madurai). The Pandyan king sent his daughter, along with 700 other noble women, craftsmen, and resources. This established the foundational blood link between Sri Lankan royalty and South Indian Chandravanshi (Turvasu) lineages. The specific king is unnamed in the Mahavamsa, referred to only as "the Pandu king at Madhura", likely an Early Sangam-era Pandyan ruler.

## 2. The Yaksha Lineage (Kuveni and the Indigenous Tribes)
*   **Role:** The original inhabitants/rulers of Sri Lanka prior to Vijaya.
*   **Details:** The Mahavamsa and local lore (like the Kohomba Kankariya) identify the original rulers as Yakshas.
    *   **Kalasena:** King of the Yaksha city of Sirisavatthu (Kuveni's uncle/grandfather figure).
    *   **Kuveni:** A Yakshini princess who aided Vijaya in conquering the other Yakshas. She became his first wife.
    *   **Jivahatta & Disala:** The son and daughter of Vijaya and Kuveni.
    *   **The Veddha Tribe:** After Kuveni was killed by her own people for betrayal, Jivahatta and Disala fled into the forests (Sumanakuta/Adam's Peak region), married each other, and their descendants became the "Veddha" (indigenous hunter-gatherer) tribe of Sri Lanka.

## 3. The Kalinga & Avanti Connection (The Sacred Tooth Relic)
*   **Role:** The protectors and transmitters of the Buddha's Sacred Tooth Relic.
*   **Textual Source:** The *Dathavamsa*.
*   **Lineage of Kalinga (Relic Keepers):**
    *   **Brahmadatta:** The King of Kalinga who received the tooth from the monk Khema Thera immediately after Buddha's parinirvana.
    *   **Guhasiva:** A later King of Kalinga in this line, who possessed the relic when the kingdom was under attack.
    *   **Princess Hemamala:** Daughter of King Guhasiva.
*   **The Avanti Connection:**
    *   **Prince Dantha:** A prince from Ujjeni (Avanti/Ujjain) who came to Kalinga out of devotion to the relic. King Guhasiva married Hemamala to him. Together, disguised as Brahmins, they smuggled the tooth relic to Sri Lanka.

## 4. The Mauryan Alliance (Religious Diplomatic Link)
*   Later, during the reign of King Devanampiya Tissa of Sri Lanka, Emperor Ashoka (Maurya) served as a massive political and religious patron, sending his son Mahinda and daughter Sanghamitta to establish Buddhism on the island.

## 5. The Chola Invasions (Political Conflict)
*   **Elara the Chola:** A Chola general/king who invaded Sri Lanka and ruled Anuradhapura for 44 years. Despite being an invader, the Mahavamsa praises him as a highly just and righteous ruler before he was defeated by the Sinhala hero Dutugamunu.
