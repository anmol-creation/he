1. Kushwaha / Koeri / Kachhi / Shakya / Maurya: The Kushwaha (Koeri, Kachhi) agricultural communities claim descent from Kusha (Suryavansh). This sanskritization movement began in the early 20th century.
2. Kadva Patidars: A prominent community in Gujarat claiming descent from Kusha (with Leva Patidars claiming descent from Lava).
3. Maurya Rajputs (Mori Rajputs): Historic rulers of Chittor.
4. Jamwal/Minhas Rajputs: Rulers of Jammu, tracing back to Agnigarbha (descendant of Kusha).
