# Planetary Ground Reality (The Truth Matrix) - Logic & Discussion Memory

This document summarizes the core logic and step-by-step rules discussed for upgrading the Kundali Engine from basic mathematical scoring to a strict, combination-based Vedic astrological framework (The 31-Point Matrix).

## Core Philosophy: The Difference Between Math and Reality
A planet may look powerful mathematically (e.g., Exalted / +5 score), but its actual output depends entirely on its **Intent (Functional Lordship)**, **Environment (Ashtakvarga/Kartari)**, and **Modifiers (Aspects/Yuti)**.
A Malefic exalted planet will often use its massive power to destroy rather than build, unless specific Vedic combinations (like Vipreet Rajyoga or Neechbhanga) cancel that negativity.

## The 7 Core Layers of Evaluation

1. **Basic State & Power (The Foundation)**
   - **Rashigat Sthiti:** Exalted (Uccha), Mooltrikona, Swarashi, Friendly, Enemy, Debilitated (Neecha).
   - **Bhavgat Sthiti:** House placement (Kendra, Trikona vs Dusthana 6,8,12).
   - **Avastha (Battery Level):** Mrit (0%), Vriddha (10%), Yuva (100%), Kumar (50%), Bal (25%). Combust (Ast) instantly drops effective material power to near 0%.
   - **Vakri (Retrograde):** Represents past-life stubborn karma.

2. **Functional Nature (Lagna Intent / Niyat)**
   - **Dost (Benefic):** Trikonesh (1st, 5th, 9th Lords).
   - **Dushman (Malefic):** Trik Lords (6th, 8th, 12th) and Marakas (2nd, 7th).
   - **Neutral:** Kendra Lords (unless kendradhipati dosha applies).
   - *Logic:* The intent dictates *how* the planet behaves when it aspects others.

3. **Nakshatra & Star Lord (The Executor)**
   - A planet acts based on the script of its Nakshatra Swami (Star Lord).
   - If a planet is Exalted, but its Nakshatra Lord is in the 8th house, the result ultimately leads to struggle.

4. **Drishti & Yuti (The Modifiers & Curse/Sanjeevani)**
   - **Sanjeevani:** A pure Benefic (e.g., Jupiter) aspecting a dead/debilitated planet revives it.
   - **Curse (Shrap/Grahan):** Rahu/Ketu/Malefic Saturn aspecting a positive planet restricts its output.
   - **Deep Yuti (Orb):** Conjunctions are only effective within ~15 degrees.
   - **Graha Yuddha (Planetary War):** Planets within 1 degree fight; the one with the lesser degree wins (Nodes and Sun excluded).
   - **Kartari Dosha (Hemming):** Malefics in the 2nd and 12th house from a planet trap it (Paap Kartari).

5. **Divisional Integrity (D-9 Navamsha)**
   - Vargottam (same sign in D-1 and D-9) multiplies strength.
   - A D-1 Exalted planet becoming Debilitated in D-9 indicates a hollow foundation.

6. **Ashtakvarga (The Environment / SAV)**
   - Sarvashtakvarga points dictate the "land quality" where the planet sits.
   - >30 points = Strong Support; <25 points = Deep Struggle.

## The Final Verdict Logic (Conditional, Not Additive)
Instead of adding +5 and -5, the engine uses strict combinations to declare the ground reality:

*   **Combust + Resurrected:** "Physically weak but Spiritually saved (Late Success)"
*   **Malefic in Dusthana (6,8,12) owning Dusthana:** "Vipreet Rajyoga (Success through crisis)"
*   **Debilitated + Resurrected:** "Neechbhanga Rajyoga (Extreme struggle to legendary success)"
*   **Pure Benefic + Exalted (Without Curses):** "Supreme Benefic (Massive Positive Gains)"
*   **Pure Benefic + Exalted (With Curses):** "Highly Favorable but Severely Restricted"
*   **Malefic + Exalted:** "Dangerous Power (High Authority - can destroy)"

*   **Saturn's Aspect on Jupiter:** Because Saturn's base intent for Cancer Lagna is *Malefic*, its aspect on Jupiter operates as a **Curse (Shrap)**. It restricts Jupiter, forcing delays and strict discipline. The Neechbhanga makes Saturn powerful, but it does *not* make Saturn "sweet" or benefic to Jupiter. A powerful enemy hits harder.
