# Comparative Mythology & Ancient History: Middle-East, Vedic & Abrahamic Roots

This document compiles the detailed discussions regarding the historical, mythological, and genealogical connections between the ancient Middle-East (Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian), Vedic/Hindu traditions, and Abrahamic theology.

## 1. Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham) & His Roots
*   **Lineage (Shem):** Ibrahim belongs to the Semitic lineage, tracing back through Terah (Azar), Nahor, Serug, Reu, Peleg, Eber, Shelah, Arpachshad, Shem, to Prophet Nuh (Noah).
*   **Azar (Terah):** Ibrahim's father (or uncle) was a renowned sculptor and merchant in the city of Ur (Babylonian/Sumerian empire) who made idols of planetary gods.
*   **Location & Time:** Lived during the Bronze Age (approx. 2000 BCE - 1800 BCE) in Ur/Mesopotamia during the transition from the Sumerian to the Old Babylonian empire.
*   **Wives & Branches:**
    *   *Sarah (Cousin):* Mother of Isaac (Ishaq) -> Origin of Bani Israel (Jewish/Christian lineage).
    *   *Hajar (Egyptian):* Mother of Ishmael (Ismail) -> Origin of the Arab lineage (Prophet Muhammad).
    *   *Keturah (Qanturah):* Mother of Midian (Madyan).

## 2. Babylonian & Sumerian Planetary Gods (Navagraha Parallels)
Azar carved anthropomorphic idols of planetary deities which closely mirror Vedic and Greek gods.
*   **Sun (Shamash/Utu):** God of Justice.
*   **Moon (Sin/Nanna):** God of Wisdom. Chief deity of the city of Ur. Parallels with Shiva (Chandrashekhara).
*   **Venus (Ishtar/Inanna):** Goddess of Love and War. Rode a lion, armed with weapons. Exact parallel to **Maa Durga**.
*   **Jupiter (Marduk):** King of the Gods, wielder of the thunderbolt/spear, slayer of the dragon Tiamat. Exact parallel to **Indra**.
*   **Saturn (Ninurta):** God of Agriculture and War.

## 3. The Origin of Adam & Eve (Theology vs. Archaeology)
### Theological View (Abrahamic)
*   Adam and Eve had no parents. Created directly from clay by the One God (Allah/Yahweh).
*   **Satan (Iblis/Azazil):** A Jinn created from smokeless fire. Refused to bow to the clay-made Adam due to pride and was cast out.
*   **Pre-Adamite Earth:** Ruled by Jinn societies who caused bloodshed until God sent an army of Angels to cleanse the earth.

### Archaeological/Mythological View (Sumerian Origins)
*   **Adapa (Adam Prototype):** Created in the city of Eridu by **Enki** (God of Wisdom/Water) and **Ninhursag** (Mother Goddess) by mixing blood and clay to serve the gods (Anunnaki).
*   **Ninti (Eve Prototype):** Created to heal Enki's ailing rib. "Ninti" in Sumerian translates to "Lady of the Rib" and "Lady of Life" (which was later literally translated by Hebrews to create the Eve narrative).
*   **The Forbidden Fruit:** Anu (King of Gods) offered Adapa the "Food of Immortality". Enki warned Adapa not to eat it, tricking him into remaining mortal. This is the origin of the Garden of Eden fruit narrative.

## 4. The Vedic Connection: Mitanni & Kassite Empires
The ancient Middle-East had a massive, undeniable Indo-Aryan/Vedic presence.
*   **The Mitanni Empire (~1500 BCE - 1300 BCE):**
    *   Ruled by a Vedic Kshatriya (warrior) elite class called the *Maryannu*.
    *   **Kings:** Kirta, Shuttarna, Parshatatar, Tushratta, Shattiwaza.
    *   **Boghazkoi Treaty:** A peace treaty signed with the Hittites invoking **Mitra, Varuna, Indra, and Nasatya (Ashvins)**.
    *   **Kikkuli Text:** A horse training manual using pure Sanskrit terms (*aika, tera, panza*).
    *   **Amarna Letters:** Correspondence between Mitanni King Tushratta and Egyptian Pharaohs proving their immense political power.
*   **The Kassite Empire (~1700 BCE in Babylon):**
    *   Even older than the Mitanni, they ruled Babylon.
    *   Worshipped Vedic gods: **Surias (Surya), Maruttas (Marut), Bugas (Bhaga)**.

## 5. The Moon God (Sin) & The Chandravansh Connection
*   The prominence of the Moon God (Sin) in Sumeria (older/superior to the Sun) mirrors the Vedic **Chandravansh (Lunar Dynasty)**.
*   Chandravanshi kings (Chandra -> Budh -> Pururavas -> Ayu -> Nahusha).
*   **Nahusha's Parallel:** Nahusha became so powerful he temporarily ruled heaven as *Indra*. This symbolizes a historical point where the Lunar dynasty (Chandravanshis) dominated the political known world, corresponding to the widespread cultural dominance of Moon-worshipping empires in the Middle-East. Many historians suggest the Anu dynasty (descendants of Yayati) migrated Westward to establish these very cultures.
