Apart from Kachhwaha Rajputs (and their sub-clans) and Agarwals, who else claims descent from Kusha?
1. Kushwaha / Koeri / Kachhi / Shakya / Maurya: Agricultural and cultivator communities in North India claim descent from Kusha.
2. Maurya Dynasty (Chandragupta Maurya): Some Rajput and community claims try to link the Mauryas to the Moriya/Maurya Rajputs, who in turn claim Suryavanshi (Kusha) descent, though historical evidence puts Mauryas as separate. But in folklore, the Maurya (Mori) Rajputs of Chittor (like Chitrangad Mori who built Chittorgarh) claim Suryavanshi descent.
3. Kadwa Patidar: A community in Gujarat claiming descent from Kusha.
4. Bais Rajputs: Sometimes claim descent from the Solar Dynasty (Suryavansh), but usually traced differently.
5. Minhas / Manhas Rajputs: Found in Jammu & Kashmir / Punjab. They are Suryavanshi Jamwal Rajputs. They trace back to Kusha. The Jamwals (rulers of Jammu) claim descent from Kusha through Agnigarbha (a descendant of Kush).
6. Sikarwar Rajputs: Claim descent from Kusha.
