# Historical Research: Karhade Brahmins, Rani Lakshmibai & The Newalkar Dynasty

This document compiles the research discussed regarding the lineage, gotras, and historical context of Rani Lakshmibai (born Manikarnika Tambe) and her marital family, the Newalkar dynasty of Jhansi.

## 1. Understanding the Terms: Vansh, Gotra, and Surname

To accurately map historical Indian figures, especially Brahmins, it is crucial to distinguish between Vansh, Gotra, and Surnames.

*   **Vansh (Lineage/Bloodline):** While Kshatriyas trace their lineage to divine or royal founders (e.g., Suryavansh from Surya/Ikshvaku, Chandravansh from Chandra/Ila), Brahmins trace their direct biological lineage to the ancient *Rishis* (Saptarishis + Agastya). Therefore, all Brahmins ultimately fall under the **Brahma Vansh** (or Rishi Vansh), as these Rishis were mind-born sons (Manas Putra) of Lord Brahma.
*   **Gotra (The True Bloodline):** For Brahmins, the Gotra *is* the actual genetic bloodline. It denotes the specific ancient Rishi from whom the family is biologically descended (e.g., Gautam, Vashistha, Kashyap).
*   **Surname (Upanam):** Surnames in the modern sense did not exist in ancient times. They are relatively recent "tags" (800-1000 years old) based on:
    *   **Geography:** The village or region they hailed from (e.g., ending in '-kar' in Maharashtra).
    *   **Profession:** Their traditional occupation (e.g., Kulkarni, Joshi, Tambe).
    *   **Knowledge:** Expertise in scriptures (e.g., Trivedi).

## 2. Who are the Karhade Brahmins?

*   **Regional Identity:** "Karhade" is not a bloodline; it is a geographical identity.
*   **Origin:** It refers to Brahmins who originally hailed from the ancient city of **Karhatak** (modern-day **Karad** in Satara district, Maharashtra). Those who migrated from Karad to regions like the Konkan (Ratnagiri, Goa) were identified as Karhade Brahmins.
*   They are one of the prominent Brahmin sub-castes in Maharashtra, alongside Deshastha and Chitpavan Brahmins. Historically, they held high administrative and military positions in the Maratha Empire and under the Peshwas.

## 3. The Newalkar Dynasty (Marital Family of Rani Lakshmibai)

*   **Background:** The Newalkar family were Karhade Brahmins originally from a village named 'Kot' in the Ratnagiri district of Maharashtra. The surname "Newalkar" likely derives from a geographical association (perhaps a village named Newal).
*   **Rise to Power:** They served the Maratha Empire. Recognizing their loyalty and administrative skills, the Peshwas appointed them as Subedars (Governors) of Jhansi in the Bundelkhand region. Later, under British paramountcy, they were granted the title of Maharaja.
*   **Gotra (Bloodline):** According to Karhade Brahmin records, the Newalkar surname is primarily associated with the **Gautam (गौतम)** and **Vashistha (वशिष्ठ)** gotras.
    *   If Gautam, their true bloodline traces to Maharishi Gautam (descendant of Maharishi Angira).
    *   If Vashistha, their true bloodline traces directly to Maharishi Vashistha.
*   **Prominent Figure:** Maharaja Gangadhar Rao Newalkar (Husband of Rani Lakshmibai).

## 4. The Tambe Family (Birth Family of Rani Lakshmibai)

*   **Background:** The Tambe family were also Karhade Brahmins. Rani Lakshmibai's father, Moropant Tambe, worked in the court of the exiled Peshwa Baji Rao II in Bithoor.
*   **Surname Origin:** The surname "Tambe" likely originated from an ancestral profession related to copper (tamba) vessels or trade.
*   **Gotra (Bloodline):** In the Karhade Brahmin community, families with the Tambe surname belong to one of four gotras:
    1.  **Gaargya (गार्ग्य):** Lineage of Maharishi Garg.
    2.  **Gautam (गौतम):** Lineage of Maharishi Gautam.
    3.  **Mudgal (मुद्गल):** Lineage of Maharishi Mudgal (descendant of Angira).
    4.  **Shaandilya (शांडिल्य):** Lineage of Maharishi Shandilya (descendant of Kashyap).
*   **Prominent Figure:** Manikarnika Tambe (Manu/Chhabili), who became Rani Lakshmibai upon her marriage to Gangadhar Rao.

## 5. Representation in the Database

Because both the Newalkars and Tambes are Brahmins tracing their lineage to the original Rishis, they have been placed under the `brahma` base node in the dataset (`js/data/historic-map-data/brahma/jhansi-newalkar.js`).

To accurately reflect Hindu marital traditions, Rani Lakshmibai's data node represents her dual gotra identity:
*   **Birth Gotra:** Gaargya / Gautam / Mudgal / Shaandilya (from the Tambe family).
*   **Marital Gotra:** Gautam / Vashistha (adopted from the Newalkar family upon marriage).
