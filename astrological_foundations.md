# Astrological Foundations: Houses, Planets, and Signs

This document maps the core logic of Vedic Astrology, categorizing the impacts and significations of the 12 Houses (Bhavas), 9 Planets (Grahas), and 12 Zodiac Signs (Rashis) for use in the Kundali Engine's advanced calculations.

## 1. The 12 Houses (Bhavas) - Categorized Significations

### 1.1. Relationships & Family
*   **1st House:** Self (The foundation of all relationships).
*   **2nd House:** Immediate Family (Kutumb), lineage.
*   **3rd House:** Younger siblings, neighbors.
*   **4th House:** Mother.
*   **5th House:** Children (Progeny), romantic partners (Love affairs).
*   **6th House:** Maternal uncle/aunt, enemies, competitors.
*   **7th House:** Spouse (Husband/Wife), business partners.
*   **8th House:** In-laws.
*   **9th House:** Gurus/Mentors, Father (in some contexts).
*   **10th House:** Father (as authority/boss).
*   **11th House:** Elder siblings, friends, social network.
*   **12th House:** Hidden enemies, betrayers.

### 1.2. Career, Wealth & Finance
*   **2nd House:** Bank balance, savings, liquid cash, assets.
*   **3rd House:** Self-made wealth (Hustle), freelancing, earnings via communication/marketing.
*   **4th House:** Fixed assets (Real estate, land, vehicles).
*   **5th House:** Speculative wealth (Stock market, trading).
*   **6th House:** Daily job (9-to-5 service), loans, debt.
*   **7th House:** Business (Open market), clients, partnership revenue.
*   **8th House:** Sudden wealth (Inheritance, lottery, insurance), bankruptcy.
*   **10th House:** Core career, profession, status, brand value.
*   **11th House:** Profits, ROI, regular income, fulfillment of financial desires.
*   **12th House:** Expenses, foreign investments, financial losses.

### 1.3. Psychology & Mindset
*   **1st House:** Ego, confidence, self-perception.
*   **3rd House:** Courage, risk-taking ability, motivation.
*   **4th House:** Inner peace, emotional security (affliction causes depression).
*   **6th House:** Stress, anxiety, fighting spirit/resilience.
*   **8th House:** Deep fears, trauma, phobias, overthinking, secrets.
*   **9th House:** Belief system, optimism, faith.
*   **12th House:** Subconscious mind, imagination, letting go (Vairagya).

### 1.4. Education & Skills
*   **2nd House:** Basic sanskars, early learning.
*   **3rd House:** Hands-on skills, coding, writing, media.
*   **4th House:** Schooling (up to 12th grade), formal degrees.
*   **5th House:** IQ, creativity (UI/UX), learning capacity.
*   **8th House:** Deep research, occult sciences (Astrology), complex logic.
*   **9th House:** Higher education (Masters/PhD), philosophy.

### 1.5. Body & Health
*   **1st House:** Overall physical body, head, immunity.
*   **2nd House:** Face, right eye, teeth.
*   **3rd House:** Shoulders, arms, vocal cords.
*   **4th House:** Chest, heart.
*   **5th House:** Stomach, spine.
*   **6th House:** Intestines, acute diseases, injuries.
*   **7th House:** Kidneys, lower back.
*   **8th House:** Private parts, chronic/incurable diseases, longevity.
*   **12th House:** Left eye, feet, hospitalization, sleep disorders.

### 1.6. Places & Travel
*   **3rd House:** Short trips, neighborhood.
*   **4th House:** Homeland, own house.
*   **7th House:** Foreign land (initial interactions).
*   **9th House:** Long-distance travel, pilgrimages.
*   **12th House:** Permanent foreign settlement, prisons, isolation/ashrams.


## 2. The 9 Planets (Grahas) - Symbols, Nature & Significations

**The Golden Rule of Planetary Analysis:**
1. **Pratik (Core Symbol):** This is universal. The Sun is always the Soul, Saturn is always Karma. This never changes.
2. **Placement (House/Sign):** This dictates *where* and *how* the Pratik manifests. (e.g., Sun in 10th = Soul is attached to Career).
3. **Strength (Avastha/Degrees):** Determines the *Intensity* or *Volume* of the impact. A strong planet gives a massive impact, weak gives a negligible impact.
4. **Positivity/Negativity:** Determined by the lordship and placement, not just strength. A strong malefic in a bad house causes intense damage.

### 2.1. Surya (Sun)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Atma (Soul), King.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Cruel/Fierce, Hot, Authoritative, Masculine.
*   **Impacts:** Father, government, leadership, ego, self-respect, bones, heart, right eye, immunity.

### 2.2. Chandra (Moon)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Man (Mind), Queen.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Gentle/Benefic (with Paksha Bala), Cold, Feminine, Fickle.
*   **Impacts:** Mother, masses, emotions, psychology, liquid cash, bodily fluids, left eye, chest.

### 2.3. Mangal (Mars)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Urja (Energy), Commander.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Malefic, Aggressive, Action-oriented, Masculine.
*   **Impacts:** Courage, anger, siblings, real estate, engineering, logic/arguments, blood, muscles.

### 2.4. Budh (Mercury)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Buddhi (Intellect), Prince.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Neutral/Adaptive, Youthful.
*   **Impacts:** Business, communication, coding logic, writing, nervous system, skin, youth, cousins.

### 2.5. Guru (Jupiter)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Gyan (Wisdom), Minister.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Most Benefic, Expansive, Sattvic, Masculine.
*   **Impacts:** Mentors, wealth, progeny, law, advisory, optimism, fat, liver.

### 2.6. Shukra (Venus)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Sukh/Bhoga (Luxury & Pleasure), Minister (of Asuras).
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Benefic, Luxurious (Rajasic), Feminine.
*   **Impacts:** Spouse/Wife, romance, UI/UX design, aesthetics, vehicles, reproductive system, facial glow.

### 2.7. Shani (Saturn)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Karma & Nyaya (Actions & Justice), Servant.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Strict Malefic, Cold, Slow, Neutral Gender.
*   **Impacts:** Discipline, delay, boundaries, reality-checks, heavy industries, routine jobs, elderly, joints, teeth.

### 2.8. Rahu (North Node)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Bhram (Illusion & Obsession), Outcast.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Malefic, Airy, Explosive Expansion.
*   **Impacts:** Internet, SaaS, AI, foreign elements, out-of-box thinking, greed, unknown fears.

### 2.9. Ketu (South Node)
*   **Pratik (Symbol):** Vairagya (Detachment & Liberation), Monk.
*   **Swabhaw (Nature):** Malefic (Fiery/Cutting).
*   **Impacts:** Deep research, microscopic work, spiritual detachment, intuition, surgery/cuts, maternal grandfather.

## 3. The 12 Zodiac Signs (Rashis) - Elements, Modality & Traits

Zodiac signs dictate the *flavor* or *environment* in which a planet operates. They are categorized by Element (Tattva) and Modality (Swabhaw).

**The Elements (Tattva):**
*   **Fire (Agni):** Action, passion, energy, aggression, initiation. (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
*   **Earth (Prithvi):** Practicality, stability, material focus, grounding. (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
*   **Air (Vayu):** Intellect, communication, movement, social networking. (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)
*   **Water (Jal):** Emotions, intuition, empathy, adaptability, hidden depths. (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)

**The Modalities (Swabhaw):**
*   **Movable (Chara):** Initiators, dynamic, restless, prone to quick changes. (1, 4, 7, 10)
*   **Fixed (Sthira):** Stable, stubborn, persistent, maintaining the status quo. (2, 5, 8, 11)
*   **Dual (Dvisvabhava):** Adaptable, flexible, sometimes confused or indecisive. (3, 6, 9, 12)

### 3.1. Aries (Mesha) - Lord: Mars
*   **Traits:** Fire + Movable.
*   **Characteristics:** Impulsive, pioneer, aggressive, independent, fast-paced. Head first.

### 3.2. Taurus (Vrishabha) - Lord: Venus
*   **Traits:** Earth + Fixed.
*   **Characteristics:** Stubborn, materialistic, appreciates luxury, steady, reliable, slow to anger but dangerous when provoked.

### 3.3. Gemini (Mithuna) - Lord: Mercury
*   **Traits:** Air + Dual.
*   **Characteristics:** Communicative, intellectual, scattered energy, adaptable, curious, two-faced/multitasking.

### 3.4. Cancer (Karka) - Lord: Moon
*   **Traits:** Water + Movable.
*   **Characteristics:** Deeply emotional, nurturing, fiercely protective of home/family, moody, holds onto the past.

### 3.5. Leo (Simha) - Lord: Sun
*   **Traits:** Fire + Fixed.
*   **Characteristics:** Royal ego, proud, natural leader, attention-seeking, fiercely loyal, dramatic.

### 3.6. Virgo (Kanya) - Lord: Mercury
*   **Traits:** Earth + Dual.
*   **Characteristics:** Analytical, critical, perfectionist, service-oriented, practical, prone to worrying.

### 3.7. Libra (Tula) - Lord: Venus
*   **Traits:** Air + Movable.
*   **Characteristics:** Balanced, diplomatic, relationship-focused, appreciates aesthetics, indecisive, seeks harmony in markets.

### 3.8. Scorpio (Vrishchika) - Lords: Mars (Traditional), Ketu
*   **Traits:** Water + Fixed.
*   **Characteristics:** Intensely secretive, transformative, vengeful, profound psychological depth, powerful intuition.

### 3.9. Sagittarius (Dhanu) - Lord: Jupiter
*   **Traits:** Fire + Dual.
*   **Characteristics:** Philosophic, optimistic, brutally honest, loves freedom/travel, preachy, dislikes restrictions.

### 3.10. Capricorn (Makara) - Lord: Saturn
*   **Traits:** Earth + Movable.
*   **Characteristics:** Ambitious, strictly disciplined, cold practicality, status-conscious, relentless worker.

### 3.11. Aquarius (Kumbha) - Lords: Saturn (Traditional), Rahu
*   **Traits:** Air + Fixed.
*   **Characteristics:** Rebellious, humanitarian, innovative (tech/SaaS), detached, highly intellectual but stubborn in beliefs.

### 3.12. Pisces (Meena) - Lord: Jupiter
*   **Traits:** Water + Dual.
*   **Characteristics:** Dreamy, highly intuitive, compassionate, escapist, spiritually inclined, lacks boundaries.

## 4. Synthesis Example: The Brutal Reality of Interpretation

To interpret a chart accurately, we combine the three pillars:
**Planet (Pratik/Nature) + Sign (Flavor/Environment) + House (Area of Life)**

**Example from Chart (Cancer Ascendant, Exalted Mars in Capricorn in 7th House):**
*   **Ascendant: Cancer (Water + Movable):** The individual is highly emotionally perceptive, protective (like a crab's shell), and their mind moves quickly. Their core focus (1st House) is on emotional and foundational security.
*   **Mars (Aggressive Energy) in Capricorn (Earth/Practical + Movable) in 7th House (Partnerships/Business):**
    *   *The Pratik:* Mars is raw energy and aggression.
    *   *The Environment:* Capricorn channels this energy. Instead of explosive anger (like in Aries), it becomes cold, calculated, relentless ambition and strict discipline.
    *   *The Area:* 7th House dictates that this intense, practical drive is directed towards open market business, public dealings, and partnerships.
    *   *Result:* The person is a highly driven, practical entrepreneur who does not entertain emotional nonsense in business.
