# Sanatan Global Historical Research & Ancestral Connections

This document serves as a memory bank for deep historical, mythological, and global ancestral research discussed for the Sanatan Mapping Project. It contains data that can be used for future lineage expansions, tooltip descriptions, or historical parichay (introductions).

## 1. Origins of Mlecchas (Global Migrations)
In Sanatan texts, "Mleccha" is not a single family tree but a socio-cultural term for tribes (originally Kshatriyas) who lost touch with Vedic rites (Vratyas) due to migration or curses.
* **Yayati's Curse:** Sons Druhyu (Bhojas/NW Mlecchas), Anu (Mlecchas), and Turvasu (Yavanas/Greeks) were cursed. King Suchetas (Druhyu's line) had 100 sons who migrated to Central Asia/Afghanistan.
* **Manu Smriti (10.43-44):** Clearly states that tribes like Yavanas (Greeks), Chinas (Chinese), Shakas (Scythians), Pahlavas (Persians), and Kambojas were originally Kshatriyas who sank to Mleccha status due to loss of rites.
* **King Sagara:** Defeated foreign tribes (Shakas, Yavanas) but spared their lives, stripping them of Vedic rights and changing their appearances, making them Mlecchas.
* **Ikshvaku Expansion:** Ikshvaku had 100 sons; 50 ruled Uttarapatha (North/Central Asia) and 48 ruled Dakshinapatha (South). Disconnected from Aryavarta, many became Vratya Kshatriyas.

## 2. Lost Knowledge & Preservation (Nalanda & Tibet)
* **Nalanda (Dharmaganja):** Destroyed by Bakhtiyar Khalji. It held royal chronicles (Vamshavalis) that documented the "Dark Age" (Kaliyuga's first 2000 years between Mahabharata and Mauryas).
* **Takshashila (Taxila):** Destroyed by the White Huns (Alchon Huns) under King Mihirakula in the 5th/6th Century AD.
* **Tibetan Preservation:** Fleeing monks took manuscripts to Tibet. The **Kangyur** and **Tengyur** are massive Tibetan translations of lost Indian texts.
* **Sakya Monastery:** In 2003, a sealed wall revealed 84,000 intact ancient scrolls, potentially containing lost Sanskrit history and sciences.
* **Sri Lanka:** The *Mahavamsa* preserved Mauryan and Buddhist history post-543 BC.

## 3. East Asian & SE Asian Deep Connections
* **Nagas and Dragons:** Sanatan 'Nagas' (water/cloud-dwelling, wealth-protecting shape-shifters) conceptually match exactly with Chinese 'Long' and Tibetan 'Lu' (Dragons).
* **China (Pre-213 BC):** Ancient Chinese myth features creators Fuxi and Nuwa, depicted as half-human, half-snake (Naga), mirroring Prajapati/Kashyapa creation myths. Pangu hatched from a cosmic egg (Hiranyagarbha).
* **Korea (Samguk Yusa):** Written in 1281, it records that in 48 AD, Princess Suriratna (Heo Hwang-ok) from "Ayuta" (Ayodhya) traveled to Korea and married King Suro. Millions of Koreans trace their lineage to Ayodhya.
* **Japan (Kojiki & Shingon Buddhism):** Vedic gods are perfectly preserved: Saraswati (Benzaiten), Shiva (Daikokuten), Kubera (Bishamonten), Brahma (Bonten), Garuda (Karura). The Vedic Homa (Yagya) is practiced as the 'Goma' ritual.
* **Tibet's First King:** The Yarlung Dynasty's first king (Nyatri Tsenpo) is recorded in Tibetan chronicles as an Indian prince (sometimes linked to Mahabharata's Rupati or the Ikshvaku clan) who crossed the Himalayas. (Note: 'Sakya' monastery means 'Grey Earth', not Shakya clan).
* **South-East Asia (Thailand/Cambodia):** Early empires like Funan began when an Indian Brahmin/Kshatriya named **Kaundinya** married a local Naga princess named **Soma**.

## 4. Seven Sisters (NE India) Vamshavalis
* **Assam (Kamarupa):** The ancient Varman kings (like Bhaskaravarman) traced their strict lineage to Naraka and Bhagadatta (son of Lord Vishnu's Varaha avatar and Bhumi).
* **Tripura:** The royal chronicle 'Rajmala' traces the Tripuri kings to Druhyu (son of Yayati).
* **Manipur:** Kings trace their lineage to Babruvahana, the son of Arjuna and the Naga princess Ulupi.
